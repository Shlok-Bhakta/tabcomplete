## Next-edit data and protocol

The bounded supervised data has 4,096 distinct synthetic training states, 1,024 development states, and 1,024 separate held-out states grouped by language/action/family. Its train/development/held-out SHA-256 values are in the frozen plan and the private data manifest. Each state includes a reconstructible recent synthetic edit, a current file, cursor and byte region, and one of insertion, replacement, deletion, or defensible no-edit targets. Applying each target reconstructs the generated after-state. No benchmark, sealed personal corpus, employer code, or unrelated later edits entered training. The compact wire format is `N\n` plus EOS for no edit, or `R\n` plus exact replacement text and EOS for edit; an empty replacement is deletion. It maps to the existing v2 canonical action schema. Incomplete or malformed outputs are not repaired or counted as terminated.

The initial 16-case native q25 Q4 evaluator smoke produced 0 terminated compact actions and 0 exact edits; it validates the evaluator path and is too small to estimate next-edit quality. The unadapted causal screening above is likewise not next-edit evidence. The first matched adaptation allocation evaluated 512 unadapted development cases per finalist, then correctly stopped at its 64-state training-only fixture. Both models learned the `R` header, EOS, and deletions, but emitted `R` on every no-edit case. Exact loss, token, memory, and fixture outcomes are preserved in [V2 diagnostic](small_model_prototype_r1/adaptation/diagnostic-v2.json). The labeled V3 fixture at 1e-3 ran to the 96-token cutoff on every case; q25 loss rose. The simpler V4 fixture at 3e-4 produced only `N` on q3 and no terminated q25 action. These stopped attempts are preserved in [V3 diagnostic](small_model_prototype_r1/adaptation/diagnostic-v3.json) and [V4 diagnostic](small_model_prototype_r1/adaptation/diagnostic-v4.json). No probe or main pilot ran in those sessions. The [V5 diagnostic suite](small_model_prototype_r1/adaptation/fixture_suite-v4.json) keeps a 64-example, two-pass disposable check with a smaller effective batch and records actual applied optimizer steps and first-layer weight movement. It also fixes the production example order with one shared deterministic shuffle; the main effective batch remains 16 and the train/development/held-out contents stay unchanged. The identical V2 unadapted outputs are reused by [hash-locked baseline record](small_model_prototype_r1/adaptation/v2-baselines.json). The fixture runs are implementation evidence, not next-edit quality evidence.

V5 passed the disposable fixture for both finalists: each had 32 applied updates, nonzero first-layer weight change, and 32/32 exact terminated no-edit and deletion actions. Both 1e-5 and 3e-5 probes reached identical 512/512 synthetic development exact after-state results for each model; the preregistered tie rule chose 1e-5. Each main pilot restarted from its untouched source checkpoint, used one pass and 256 effective-batch-16 updates, and processed 664,948 nonpadding input tokens (prompt and response token inventories are in [progress.json](small_model_prototype_r1/adaptation/progress.json)). The process-exit reload check restored each 256-update training state. On the 1,024-case synthetic development split, both models emitted 1,024 valid terminated actions, correctly left all 256 no-edit states alone, and exactly reconstructed all 768 edit-required after-states. The split has only 16 generated task-family groups and close template relatives; the perfect result supports learning this synthetic task, not real-editor accuracy or equivalence between foundations. The models had zero discordant development outcomes. Q25 was [locked for held-out evaluation](small_model_prototype_r1/deployment/selection.json) because its measured desktop predictor RSS and warm latency were lower. Automatic mode remains ineligible without genuine displayed-proposal calibration and the latency gate.

The single locked held-out evaluation then gave q25 1,024/1,024 valid terminated actions, 768/768 exact edit-required after-states and 256/256 correct no-edit states with zero false-positive edits. Its [result and identity record](small_model_prototype_r1/deployment/heldout-result.json) matches the selection and adapted weight hashes. The held-out set is generated from the same small template grammar, so this result does not validate generalization to unpredictable human edits. No held-out-driven retuning was performed.

The first selected Q4 conversion stopped before producing weights because the Transformers 5.5 inference export wrote `extra_special_tokens` as a list, while the pinned llama.cpp converter's Transformers 4.57 tokenizer loader expects a mapping. The [failed job record](small_model_prototype_r1/deployment/conversion-job-v1.json) preserves the private log hash. Export revision 2 stages the adapted weight through a symlink and restores the exact tokenizer JSON and metadata from q25's immutable source revision, checking that the training tokenizer hash matches that source. This repairs conversion compatibility without changing the trained checkpoint, development choice, held-out score, or comparison fixtures.

## Collector audit before training readiness

A disposable multilingual repository exercised the existing collector with stubbed rare model responses. The final synthetic session `e022bfdc-1d61-400c-bc98-64ce6e3694ab` has 39 unique events, zero sequence gaps, six requests, three shown proposals, one scripted acceptance, two scripted explicit rejections, three edit deltas including undo, and eight retrievable anchor payloads whose SHA-256 values all matched. Replay reconstructed all three deltas with no unanchored or mismatched events. An interrupted collector request spooled locally and recovered. These are integration checks, not human acceptance or model-quality observations. Earlier synthetic sessions with pre-anchor gaps remain in the collector and are excluded from readiness. The evidence export keeps five-second, thirty-second, and next-save outcomes censored when unobserved; ignoring, stale responses, shadow predictions, timeouts, and explicit rejection remain distinct. There are zero defensible genuine preference pairs. Automatic personalization is disabled. See [feedback verification](small_model_prototype_r1/feedback_verification/live_synthetic.json).

A later personal adaptation would train a separate candidate adapter from a batch of verified feedback, check held-out personal and general regressions, and activate or reject a versioned artifact with rollback. This campaign does not train preferences or mutate the served model during typing pauses.

## Limits, budget, and next use

The desktop is the actual tested and installed target. The configured T480s alias timed out; the tested installer accepts a target runtime path but was not installed or measured there. On the desktop, the automatic latency gate (median at most 500 ms, p95 at most 1,000 ms) failed, and there are fewer than 50 qualified human displayed proposals. Real editing accuracy and reversal behavior remain uncalibrated. These are genuine blockers for automatic mode, even though synthetic serialization and memory pass. A user may invoke manual prediction and decide whether to accept; no human acceptance rate has been measured.

At the final authenticated Kaggle observation, 2026-09-25 10:18:55 UTC, 15.62 of 45 account-hours remained until the stated 2026-09-26 00:00 renewal, with no active jobs. This campaign used 11,422.56 GPU-session seconds (3.173 hours), including stopped diagnostics and evaluations, and 2,101,500 additional nonpadding training input tokens, below its 10-hour and 32-million-token caps. Each finalist's main pilot made one pass, 664,948 tokens. The CPU-only selected conversion used 2,395,206,210 bytes of new research artifacts including staging accounting, below 12 GiB. The only new desktop model file is the 397,807,232-byte selected Q4 GGUF, below 2 GiB; prior user checkpoints were preserved. [The budget ledger](small_model_prototype_r1/budget_ledger.json), [quota observation](small_model_prototype_r1/quota_final.json), and [download manifest](small_model_prototype_r1/download_manifest.json) distinguish quota usage from local storage. No paid model calls, external teacher model, raw-code CPT, or online weight updates occurred.

Feedback export schema `personalization-feedback-evidence-v2` preserves event provenance, ambiguity flags, and versioned hashes without promoting ignored or stale predictions to rejections. Automatic personalization remains `enabled=false`. A later batch would need at least 256 defensible preference pairs from three sessions, a temporal/session holdout, general regression cases, verified training memory, no inference contention, and a rollback artifact before training a separate candidate adapter. Idle training would additionally require a sustained idle window, power and memory checks, and cooldown; it must never mutate the served model. The candidate would be activated only after held-out personal and general checks. These thresholds are starting engineering rules, not a guarantee of improvement.
